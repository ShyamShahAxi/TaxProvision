Company & assessment

Singapore corporate tax is 17%. The partial exemption (75% of the first $10k of chargeable income + 50% of the next $190k) and start-up exemption are applied automatically. The CIT rebate is set by Budget each year — enter the rate and cap for the relevant YA.
